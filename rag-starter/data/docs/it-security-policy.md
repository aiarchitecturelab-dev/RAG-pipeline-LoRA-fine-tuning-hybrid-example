# Acme Corp IT Security Policy

This policy sets out the security rules that every employee, contractor, and intern at Acme Corp must follow when using company systems and data. The IT Security team owns this policy and reviews it every year.

## Passwords and multi-factor authentication

Passwords for company accounts must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused on any other service. Employees should use the company-approved password manager rather than writing passwords down. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN, and every system that holds customer information. Never share a password or a one-time code with anyone, including IT staff. IT Security will never ask for your password.

## Device security

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption switched on and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Security updates should be installed within 14 days of release. Personal devices may only access company email through the managed mobile app, and never store customer files locally. A lost or stolen device must be reported immediately, even if you think it may turn up.

## Phishing and suspicious email

Be careful with unexpected messages that ask you to click a link, open an attachment, or urgently transfer money. Check the sender address and hover over links before clicking. If you receive a suspicious email, do not reply and do not open attachments. Use the Report Phishing button in the email client, which sends the message to IT Security and removes it from your inbox. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, report a security incident straight away.

## Reporting a security incident

Any suspected security incident must be reported to the IT Security desk within 1 hour of discovery. Use the incident form on the intranet or email security@acme.example. Examples include a lost device, a phishing link you clicked, customer data sent to the wrong recipient, and unusual account activity. Reporting quickly is always better than reporting perfectly. Employees who report in good faith will not be penalized, even if the incident turns out to be a mistake.

## Data classification

Acme Corp uses four data classes: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Customer records, contracts, and financial results are Confidential at minimum. Restricted data, such as credentials and board papers, may only be shared with named recipients. Label documents with their class and store them only in approved company systems.

## Use of AI tools

Employees may use AI tools that have been approved by IT Security and listed on the intranet. Do not paste Confidential or Restricted data, or any customer personal information, into an AI tool that is not approved. Approved tools are configured so that prompts are not used to train external models. Treat AI output as a draft: you remain responsible for checking facts before using it in work that reaches customers.

## Remote access and VPN

Connect to internal systems only through the company VPN when working outside an Acme Corp office. Do not use public computers, such as those in hotels or libraries, to sign in to company accounts. Public Wi-Fi is acceptable only when the VPN is switched on.

## Removable media

USB storage devices are disabled on company laptops by default. If you have a business need to move files with removable media, request an exception from IT Security. Approved exceptions require an encrypted device supplied by the company.
