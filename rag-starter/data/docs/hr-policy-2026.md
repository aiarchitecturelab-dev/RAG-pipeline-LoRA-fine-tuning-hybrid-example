# Acme Corp HR Policy 2026

This policy describes working conditions and leave entitlements for all employees of Acme Corp. It applies to full-time and part-time staff in every office. If you are unsure how a rule applies to your situation, contact the People Team before making plans.

## Working hours and remote work

The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday, with core collaboration hours between 10:00 and 15:00 local time. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with the agreement of their manager. Working from another country for more than 14 days in a calendar year needs written approval from the People Team, because of tax and legal implications.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Leave accrues monthly. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year and must be used by 31 March. Requests for an absence longer than five consecutive working days should be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.

## Sick leave

Employees receive up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required after three consecutive days of absence. Sick days are not deducted from annual leave. Please tell your manager as early as possible on the first day of absence, ideally before the working day starts.

## Parental leave

Acme Corp provides 12 weeks of paid parental leave to every eligible employee who becomes a parent through birth, adoption, or foster placement. The entitlement is the same for all parents, regardless of gender or of whether they are the birth parent. Leave is paid at 100 percent of base salary.

To be eligible, an employee must have completed 6 months of continuous service before the expected start of the leave. Leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or placement of the child. It can be taken as one continuous block or as up to two blocks of at least two weeks each. Employees should give their manager and the People Team at least 30 days notice where possible.

After the paid leave ends, an employee may request up to 4 additional weeks of unpaid leave. Returning employees may also ask for a phased return over 4 weeks at reduced hours, with pay adjusted to the hours worked.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Each review covers goals agreed at the start of the period, feedback from colleagues, and a development plan. Ratings use a five-point scale and are discussed in a one-to-one meeting between the employee and their manager. Salary reviews take place once a year in April. Salary and bonus decisions for staff below director level are made by department heads within the annual budget set by Finance.

## Reporting concerns

Every employee has the right to raise a concern about conduct, safety, or ethics without fear of retaliation. Concerns can be raised with a manager, with the People Team, or anonymously through the independent Speak Up line. Reports are acknowledged within two working days. The People Team assigns an investigator who is independent of the people involved, and the person who raised the concern is told the outcome where the law allows.

## Notice periods and leaving Acme Corp

The standard notice period is one month for employees in their first two years of service and two months afterwards. Managers may agree a shorter period in writing. On the last working day, employees must return all company equipment, including laptops, badges, and any company payment cards. Unused annual leave is paid out or, where agreed, taken during the notice period.
