# Acme Corp Expense Policy

This policy explains which business costs Acme Corp reimburses, how much you may spend, and how to claim. All amounts are in US dollars. Spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own.

## What can be claimed

Acme Corp reimburses reasonable costs that are necessary for your work: transport, accommodation, meals while travelling, client hospitality, and small items of business equipment. Costs must be incurred for company business and must be supported by a receipt. Personal expenses are never reimbursed, even if they occur during a business trip.

## Spending limits

The limits below apply per person unless stated otherwise. Anything above a limit needs approval before you spend.

| Category | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Hotel | 180 per night |
| Meals while travelling | 60 per day |
| Client entertainment | 80 per person per event |
| Taxi or ride share | 50 per trip |
| Business equipment | 200 without prior approval |

## Receipts and documentation

A receipt is required for every claim above 25. Submit each claim within 30 days of the date of the expense. Claims submitted after 30 days may be rejected unless your manager confirms in writing that the delay was justified. A photo of the receipt is accepted if all details are readable. For claims without a receipt, use the missing receipt form and explain why the receipt is unavailable.

## Travel and accommodation

Book flights and hotels through the Acme Corp travel portal so that rates and safety information are tracked in one place. Fly economy class on flights shorter than six hours. Business class may be approved by a director for longer flights. Choose the standard hotel rate and avoid upgrades. Rental cars are permitted only when public transport is not a practical option.

## Client entertainment

Client meals and events need pre-approval from your manager. Include the names of all attendees, the client company, and the business purpose in your claim. The limit of 80 per person applies to food and drink together. Gifts to clients must not exceed 50 in value and must never be offered while a contract decision is pending.

## Submitting and approving claims

Submit claims in the expense portal. Your manager approves claims up to 500. A department head approves claims above 500 and up to 2,000. The finance director approves anything above 2,000. Approvers may not approve their own claims. Approved claims are reimbursed within 10 working days by bank transfer. If a claim is rejected, the portal shows the reason and you can resubmit it with corrections.

## Items that are not reimbursed

The following are not reimbursed: fines and penalties, alcohol that is not part of a client event, personal entertainment, traffic or parking tickets, airport lounge memberships, and mini-bar charges. Acme Corp also does not reimburse travel insurance for personal trips, or clothing other than safety wear required for the job.
