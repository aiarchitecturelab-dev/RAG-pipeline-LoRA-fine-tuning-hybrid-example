# Acme Corp Executive Compensation 2026

This document summarizes executive compensation at Acme Corp for the 2026 financial year. It is restricted to members of the Executive Committee and the Board. Do not forward it or quote figures from it outside those groups.

## Confidentiality

The contents of this document are classified Restricted. Access is limited to executives and to the directors who sit on the Compensation Committee. Any employee who discovers this document outside those groups must report it to IT Security within 1 hour.

## Base salary bands

Base salaries are set annually by the Compensation Committee. For 2026 the approved annual base salaries are: Chief Executive Officer 620,000; Chief Financial Officer 410,000; Chief Technology Officer 395,000; Chief Risk Officer 380,000. Other members of the Executive Committee are paid within a band of 300,000 to 360,000. Base salary is reviewed once a year in February.

## Annual bonus

The target annual bonus is 80 percent of base salary for the Chief Executive Officer and 60 percent for other executives. The bonus pool is funded by a scorecard: 50 percent financial results, 30 percent risk and compliance outcomes, and 20 percent strategic goals. Payouts can range from zero to 150 percent of target. The Compensation Committee may reduce any payout at its discretion, for example after a serious control failure.

## Long-term incentives

Executives receive annual grants of performance share units. The grant value is 150 percent of base salary for the Chief Executive Officer and 100 percent for other executives. Units vest after three years, and the number that vest depends on total shareholder return compared with a peer group. Unvested units are forfeited if an executive resigns.

## Severance and change of control

If the company ends the employment of the Chief Executive Officer without cause, or after a change of control, the severance payment is 18 months of base salary. For other executives it is 12 months of base salary. No severance is paid after dismissal for cause or after resignation without good reason.

## Clawback

Acme Corp may recover bonus and share-unit payments made in the previous three years if the financial statements are later restated or if an executive is found to have committed serious misconduct. The Board decides on any recovery after advice from the Compensation Committee and legal counsel.

## Executive benefits

Executives receive a supplemental pension contribution of 12 percent of base salary and an annual health assessment. There is no car allowance. Business travel follows the standard Expense Policy, except that the Chief Executive Officer may approve business class flights for other executives on flights of any length.

## Approval process

The Compensation Committee meets in February and October. Changes to any figure in this document need a majority vote of the Committee and are minuted. The Chair reports the outcome to the full Board at its next meeting.
